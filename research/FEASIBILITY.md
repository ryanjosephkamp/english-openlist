# Feasibility — which sources are actually obtainable, at what cost

Probed 2026-08-16. Every size below is a measured `content-length` or
`Content-Range` from the live artifact, not a remembered figure.

**Verdict: the source design is executable within the disk budget. Four things
the research plan assumed turn out otherwise, all cheap to absorb, and one
channel it promised cannot be built and has been scoped out honestly.**

**Free disk: 14 GiB** (`/System/Volumes/Data`, 97% full). This is the binding
constraint on everything below.

## Four assumptions that turned out otherwise

### 1. `wordfreq` is not installed

The research plan lists it among the already-present toolkit. It is not.
Confirmed present: numpy, scipy, sklearn, torch, statsmodels, nltk, pyarrow,
pandas, requests, tqdm. Confirmed absent: **wordfreq**, duckdb, polars.

56.8 MB wheel, pure-Python dependencies (`msgpack`, `langcodes`, `regex`,
`ftfy`). Trivial to install — but it is a new dependency and Phase 1 should say
so in its commit rather than presenting it as pre-existing.

### 2. hunspell needs an expansion step, and there is no binary

A `.dic` file is stems plus affix flags, not a word list. So *"hunspell en_US
lists X"* is ambiguous between *X is a stem* and *X is generated by a stem and an
affix rule* — and the two answers give very different record counts, which makes
it a live threat to the reproducibility gate rather than a detail.

No `hunspell` or `aspell` binary is installed, and none is needed:
**`spylls`** (0.1.7, 1.51 MB, pure Python, zero dependencies) expands `.dic` +
`.aff` to surface forms. `MANIFEST.toml` records `expansion = "spylls"` per
source so the choice is in writing.

### 3. SCOWL's tiers are nested and must not be ten detectors

The tarball contains `english-words.10` through `.95`, confirmed by listing the
archive without extracting it. These are **nested by construction** — tier 10 ⊂
tier 20 ⊂ … ⊂ tier 95 — so entering them as ten binary detectors would feed the
model ten perfectly dependent columns and wreck the identifiability check for no
information gain.

SCOWL enters as **one ordinal covariate**: the smallest tier containing the
string, or ∞.

### 4. web2 is a free, genuinely independent detector nobody counted

macOS ships `/usr/share/dict/words` → `web2`, which is **Webster's Second
International (1934)**, public domain, 234,428 entries under the pinned filter.

It is worth including precisely because its lineage is independent of every
modern source in the manifest. TWL descends from OSPD descends from Merriam-
Webster; hunspell descends from SCOWL; wordfreq aggregates over corpora already
ingested separately. web2 predates all of it. In a design whose main threat to
validity is conditional dependence, a detector with no declared edges into the
rest of the graph is unusually valuable, and it costs nothing.

Measured contribution: web2 alone moves 45,280 candidates out of the all-zero
cell that neither WordNet nor Wiktionary reaches.

## One channel that cannot be built

**"Multiple unrelated authors" is not measurable at feature time.** The
definition needs author diversity; no obtainable corpus supplies it.

| Candidate | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| OpenSubtitles (OPUS) `en.txt.gz` | 3.66 GB of monolingual text with **no document or author boundaries**. It can support token frequency and nothing else. Author diversity is not recoverable from it at any disk cost. |
| Project Gutenberg | Has per-book author metadata, but bulk retrieval is ~80 GB and the operators explicitly discourage crawling. Out of budget and out of manners. |
| Wikipedia dump | Gives article boundaries, so **document** dispersion is measurable — but a Wikipedia article has no single author, so it cannot stand in for author diversity either. |
| Google Books 1-grams | Gives `volume_count`, which is volume dispersion, not author dispersion. Two volumes can share an author. |

**Scoped accordingly, and written into the protocol rather than quietly
dropped:**

- At **feature** time, author diversity is approximated by **volume/document
  dispersion** (Gries's DP, Juilland's D, volume frequency, burstiness). The
  protocol calls this an approximation and does not claim otherwise.
- At **adjudication** time, author diversity is measured **directly** — Google
  Books carries author, title and date per snippet, which is exactly what gate
  G2 needs, and it is the reason Google Books is the first instrument in the
  fixed search order rather than the fourth.

This is a real reduction in what the feature layer can claim. It is recorded
here so the paper states it rather than a reviewer finding it.

## Licensing

**NWL2023 (NASPA) and CSW21 (Collins) are proprietary.** Both are fetchable, and
the existing dataset already draws 176,124 entries from TWL, so this is a
pre-existing condition rather than a new exposure — but a *published
reproducibility package* is a different artifact from a word list on Hugging
Face, and the distinction is worth respecting.

**Decision (2026-08-16): include as detectors, publish in aggregate only.**
`MANIFEST.toml` carries `redistributable = false` for both, which means:

- **published**: per-source sensitivity and specificity, the evidence-pattern
  table, every model result that depends on them
- **not published**: the raw per-word column for those two sources
- the reproducibility package ships a **fetch script**, not the data

Everything else in the manifest is public domain, CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, LGPL/MPL or
WordNet-3.0, and the full per-word matrix for those sources ships with the paper.

## Cost of the human arm, corrected

The 2026-08-16 rehearsals measured **20.1 s/item overall**, extrapolating to
~9.2 hours for 1,650 items. That figure blends searched and unsearched items,
and the protocol now makes searching mandatory (§8.3), so it understates.

The applicable rate is the **searched** rate: **30.4 s/item**, against 9.8 s
unsearched.

| | Presentations | Rate | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Calibration sample | 1,500 | 30.4 s | 12.7 h |
| Anchors (60 positive, 60 pseudo) | 120 | 30.4 s | 1.0 h |
| Self-consistency re-presentations | 150 | 30.4 s | 1.3 h |
| **Total** | **1,770** | | **≈ 14.9 h** |

**The always-search rule costs roughly 6 hours over the blended estimate.** It
buys the independence the Rogan–Gladen correction assumes, which is not optional,
but the price belongs in the plan rather than in Phase 4.

## What was probed, and how

Read-only throughout. `curl -sIL` for `content-length`, `curl -r 0-0` for
`Content-Range` where HEAD was unhelpful, `tar -tzf -` piped from `curl` to list
the SCOWL archive without extracting it, the PyPI JSON API for wheel sizes, the
Wikimedia directory index for dump sizes, and the Google Books Ngram Viewer JSON
endpoint for the five OCR-contamination measurements in PROTOCOL.md §2.4.

No artifact was retained. The three data files were checksummed before and after
and are byte-identical.
